ROUTINE / AM & PM

The daily ritual

Order matters more than quantity. Here's the sequence, and why each step comes where it does.

Morning

  1. 01
    Rinse, don't scrub. Skin sheds overnight; cold water is enough to clear it. Cleanser at night, not both ends of the day.
  2. 02
    Antioxidant serum, thin layer. Vitamin C works best on bare skin, before anything oil-based sits on top of it.
  3. 03
    Moisturize while damp. Locks in water instead of just sitting on the surface.
  4. 04
    SPF, always last. Everything before this is undone if you skip it. Reapply if you're outside past noon.

Evening

  1. 01
    Double cleanse if you wore SPF. Oil-based first to lift sunscreen, then a gentle wash to clear the rest.
  2. 02
    Actives on alternating nights. Retinoids and acids don't need to be nightly — skin repairs in the gaps.
  3. 03
    Richer cream, no rush. Night is the only time skin isn't fighting pollution or UV, so let a heavier layer sit.
  4. 04
    Hands off after that. Touching your face at 1am undoes step 3 more than any product choice.
